Research

Bridging process science and data science to solve real-world business problems.

Research Overview

Our lab aims to solve real-world business problems by advancing research in process science, data science, and process mining which bridges two disciplines.

  • Process Science × Data Science
  • Event-log-driven methodology
  • Real-world industrial application

Research Areas

Process Model Discovery

Extracting and constructing a comprehensive model of a business process from event logs — uncovering the actual sequence of activities rather than relying on preconceived or manual models.

  • •Development of novel discovery algorithms
  • •Optimization of existing algorithms
  • •Industrial application

Conformance Checking & Anomaly Detection

Comparing real process executions against reference models to measure adherence, and identifying unusual patterns that deviate from standard behavior — errors, fraud, or inefficiencies.

  • •Development of novel checking algorithms
  • •Optimization of existing algorithms
  • •Industrial application

Predictive Process Monitoring

Using data analysis and machine learning to anticipate future states of business processes — next activity, remaining time, outcome — based on historical and real-time data.

  • •Development of novel monitoring algorithms
  • •Optimization of existing algorithms
  • •Industrial application
