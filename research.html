Research

Research Overview

Our lab aims to solve real-world business problems by advancing research in process science, data science, and process mining which bridges two disciplines.

Research Areas

Process Model Discovery

Process model discovery is the technique of extracting and constructing a comprehensive model of a business process from event logs. This involves analyzing data from information systems to uncover the actual sequence of activities within a process, rather than relying on preconceived notions or manual modeling. Our lab not only utilizes existing process model discovery techniques but also develops novel ones.

  • Development of novel process model discovery algorithms
  • Optimization of existing process model discovery algorithms
  • Industrial application of process model discovery algorithms

Process Conformance Checking / Anomaly Detection

Process conformance checking compares an actual business process, as recorded in event logs, with a predefined reference model to determine how closely the real-life process adheres to the expected model. Process anomaly detection identifies unusual or unexpected patterns in business process executions that deviate from standard behavior.

  • Development of novel process conformance checking algorithms
  • Optimization of existing process conformance checking algorithms
  • Industrial application of process conformance checking algorithms

Predictive Process Monitoring

Predictive process monitoring uses data analysis and machine learning to anticipate future states (e.g. next activity, remaining time, outcome) of business processes based on historical and real-time data. Our lab focuses on event log encoding techniques and adapting machine / deep learning methods tailored for predictive process monitoring tasks.

  • Development of novel predictive process monitoring algorithms
  • Optimization of existing predictive process monitoring algorithms
  • Industrial application of predictive process monitoring algorithms
